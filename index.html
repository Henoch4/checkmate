Why Checkmate

Chess rules stay honest because the stakes do.

💰

Stakes escrowed on-chain

Both players lock the same wager in the contract before a single move. The winner takes 2×; nobody can walk away from a losing position with their stake.

⏳

24-hour move clock

If your opponent goes quiet for a day, claimTimeout forfeits the game to you. Stalling is not a strategy.

♟

Portable game state

Moves are recorded as standard SAN plus the resulting FEN, so the position is fully reconstructable — in this app or anywhere else.

How it works

Three steps from challenge to payout.

01

Create & stake

Name your opponent and send the wager with createGame. Your BOT sits in the contract waiting for them.

02

Join & play

The opponent matches the wager to join. Then it's ordinary chess — moves are submitted as SAN with the resulting FEN.

03

Mate or fold

Checkmate pays the winner the full pot. Resign and timeout do the same. Cancel before join refunds the creator.
